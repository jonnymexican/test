How it works

Every task is published by the fleet (admin-only) and is pure arithmetic — no downloads, no files, no network calls from task code. Each chunk is crunched by two independent nodes; results must match byte-for-byte before anyone is credited, and mismatches are requeued automatically. Your machine only ever sees math.

Tabs crunch while open. Close it anytime — your credits stay on the board.
